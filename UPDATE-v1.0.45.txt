MYFAMILY FRONTEND UPDATE — v1.0.45

NEW PARENTS’ PORTRAIT

- The dashboard welcome card now includes a tasteful gold-framed portrait area.
- Until a photograph is uploaded, it shows a clean heart placeholder.
- An administrator can upload, replace or remove the parents’ photograph under:
  Administration → Parents’ portrait.
- The administrator can also change the short caption displayed with the portrait.
- The full photograph stays in a dedicated private Google Drive folder.
- Only an optimized thumbnail is sent to the dashboard for fast loading.
- On small mobile screens the portrait remains compact and the caption hides automatically.

FAVICON

- The distinctive four-brothers shield remains the browser favicon.
- The figures have natural proportions and clearer interlocking hands/fingers.

VERSION

- Frontend: FE v1.0.45
- Required backend: BE v1.0.36

UPLOAD

Upload every file in this folder directly to the root of the MyFamily GitHub repository.
Do not upload the enclosing folder or ZIP file.
